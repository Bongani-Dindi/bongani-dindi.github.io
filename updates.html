Materials testing · Engineering Design & planning

Ultraviolet ageing chamber

I’m developing an ultraviolet ageing chamber to support materials testing and failure-analysis work. The design brings together UV lamps, specimen layout, temperature control, condensation and test documentation.

Recent focus

Defining the intended tests and requirements, planning the subsystems, and working through the early risks around lamp suitability, UV distribution and repeatable test conditions.

Next focus

Test the key design ideas and use measured irradiance and temperature behaviour to guide the build and qualification work.

  • Systems engineering
  • UV ageing
  • Test planning

Simulation tools · Software Tool development

UV lamp & irradiance calculator

Alongside the chamber, I’m building a Python tool to explore how lamp spacing, specimen distance and enclosure geometry affect irradiance across the sample plane.

Recent focus

Improving the clarity and resolution of the heat maps, showing lamp positions, keeping the view stable as inputs change, and incorporating spectral data for UVA-340 lamps.

Next focus

Compare model predictions with measurements and establish a measured lamp radiant output for absolute irradiance estimates.

  • Python
  • Heat maps
  • Spectral data
  • Model validation

Energy systems · Software Data & modelling

Solar PV & battery modelling

I’m using PV production and household demand profiles to explore battery operation, sizing and the economics of a residential energy system.

Recent focus

Synchronising household load profiles with the solar calendar, checking missing data, and preserving actual leap-day solar values while clearly labelling estimated leap-day demand.

Next focus

Verify calendar alignment and data quality, compare forecasts against observed demand, and evaluate battery scenarios.

  • Python
  • Time-series data
  • Battery simulation
  • Energy economics

Mechanical analysis · Engineering Simulation study

Piping & flange thermal-stress analysis

I’m studying how temperature changes and mechanical constraints affect stresses in a piping and flange assembly using finite element analysis.

Recent focus

Developing a global piping model and a local flange model, defining operating load cases and bolt preload assumptions, and documenting a plan for sensitivity studies.

Next focus

Check mesh convergence and compare thermal and support assumptions before interpreting stresses and flange behaviour.

  • Autodesk Fusion
  • Finite element analysis
  • Thermal loading
  • Mesh convergence
